You are a casual game designer for Saudi youth. Create a genuinely FUN, polished HTML mini-game about navigating professional/social situations through dialogue. If it feels like a language test, you failed.

## CRITICAL: Make It a Real Game
Player is living through "a day in the life" of a Saudi professional. Each dialogue choice shapes relationships and outcomes. The English expressions are what you naturally say — not grammar drills.

Think: interactive drama, visual novel lite, character relationship simulator.

## Juice Requirements (MUST implement at least 5)
1. **NPC expressions**: Each NPC has at least 3 emoji expressions (neutral, happy, surprised/disappointed) that change based on player choice
2. **Relationship indicator**: A subtle "trust/rapport" indicator that shifts with choices (not score — relationship warmth)
3. **Dialogue animations**: Bubbles slide up with stagger delay. Options appear one-by-one with increasing delay.
4. **Badge earn animation**: When completing a stage, a badge flies into the collection row with spin + glow
5. **Screen mood shifts**: Background subtly warms/cools based on conversation tone
6. **Choice ripple**: Selected option briefly pulses before NPC responds
7. **Stage transition**: Between stages, a brief "Later that day..." transition card with scene change
8. **End testimonials**: NPCs "send messages" thanking/congratulating the player based on choices

## Structure: Career Day Adventure (Multi-Screen)
3 screens with CSS fade transitions, no reloads:
- **Splash (1.5s)**: title + emoji + spinner. Fade out.
- **Game**: day adventure with stages.
- **Results**: badge gallery + NPC messages + play again.

## Mobile Game Features (REQUIRED)
**Sound** (Web Audio API, no files): sfxDialogueAppear() = soft pop. sfxChoiceSelect() = click. sfxStageComplete() = ascending arpeggio. sfxBadgeEarned() = sparkle chime.

**localStorage**: best score, badges earned, relationship levels. Key: `"saudi_game_progress"`.
