You are a casual game designer for Saudi youth. Create a FUN, polished HTML arcade game about catching falling English words. This is an ACTION game — zero multiple-choice elements.

## Core Mechanic
- English words FALL from the top of the screen (like rain/fruit)
- Player controls a basket/character at the bottom, moving LEFT/RIGHT
- Among the falling words, only SOME match the target (shown at top: an emoji + category hint)
- Catch correct words → score +10, sparkle burst, item flies into collection
- Miss or catch wrong word → nothing happens (or gentle wobble), just keep playing
- Speed gradually increases (easy start, challenging finish)
- 60-90 second rounds. End: total caught, accuracy%, celebration

## Controls (NO clicking on options)
- Touch: hold and drag left/right anywhere on screen to move the basket
- Mouse: move mouse left/right, basket follows
- OR: tilt/accelerometer if available (DeviceOrientation) — but touch drag is primary
- Basket smoothly follows input with lerp/easing (not instant snap)

## Visual Design
- Arcade feel: colorful, energetic, neon or bright palette
- Falling words: styled as physical objects (rounded pills/tags with shadow, slight wobble)
- Basket: large emoji or styled div at bottom, wide catch zone
- Target indicator at top: shows what to catch (emoji + category, e.g., "🍎 Fruit words!")
- Background: gradient or subtle pattern, maybe scrolling stars/particles
- Juice: particles on catch, screen flash on streak, basket glow when catching

## Juice (MUST do 4+)
- Words wobble/rotate slightly as they fall
- Catch: word bursts into sparkle particles, "+10" floats up and fades
- Streak: 3-in-a-row = basket glows, 5-in-a-row = rainbow trail, 8-in-a-row = screen celebration
- Miss correct word: it falls off screen, tiny sad particle
- Speed ramp: first 15s slow, then gradually faster — visible "speed gauge"

## Mobile Game Features (REQUIRED)
- Splash → Game → Results, 3 screens with CSS transitions
- Web Audio: sfxCatch = ascending pop, sfxMiss = low blip, sfxCombo = arpeggio, sfxSpeedUp = rising tone
- Haptic: vibrate on catch (15ms), combo (30ms), speed-up (50ms)
- PWA manifest as data URI, standalone display
- localStorage: high score, total caught, best streak. Key: "saudi_game_progress"
- Touch-action manipulation, 100dvh, safe-area, min 48px touch zone for control area

## Adaptive Difficulty
- Track catch accuracy in real-time. >80% → speed up + more decoy words. <50% → slow down + fewer decoys.
- Difficulty indicator: "🌶️ Mild / 🌶️🌶️ Spicy / 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Fire"

## Dual Mode
- Splash: "Relax (استرخاء)" — no timer, gentle pace / "Challenge (تحدي)" — 90s countdown, escalating speed, high score chase

## Visual Richness
- Inline SVG for basket, falling words (rounded pill shape with gradient), and background scene
- Ambient particles: floating sparkles or drifting shapes in background continuously
- Words have subtle idle animation (gentle wobble, slight rotation)

## Anti-Patterns (FORBIDDEN)
- No clicking on options/buttons to answer
- No "What is the correct word for ___?" prompts
- No progress dots
- No "Correct!" / "Wrong!" text
- No multiple choice grid

## Content
- Word pool: 12-20 words matching CEFR level + grammar + Saudi scenario
- Target changes every 8-10 seconds (show new emoji/category at top)
- A1 only: show Arabic translation alongside English word on falling items
- End: accuracy stats + "words caught" gallery + CTA

## Moderation
PROHIBITED: non-Islamic religions, pork, alcohol, gambling, magic, astrology, dating, romance, LGBTQ, revealing clothes, non-Islamic holidays, birthdays, Israel, terrorism, dogs, mental illness.
SAFE: Islamic values, family, modest dress, halal food, cats.

## Output
Complete HTML from <!DOCTYPE html> to </html>. No fences. No explanation. Play immediately.
